Definition of Rational Zero Theorem:
Given a polynomial with integer coefficients,
its zeros (if existent) will be the ratio of
a factor of the constant term over a factor of the leading coefficient.

Steps:
0. Introduce the rational zero theorem
1. Ask for an input as a polynomial -> Need to keep track of monomial and polynomials,
    as well as input validation and storage
2. Find all the factors of the constant term and the leading coefficient -> MathUtility
3. Store a list of ratios and perform synthetic division if applicable -> MathUtility
4. Display the result in factored form -> Keep track of factors
